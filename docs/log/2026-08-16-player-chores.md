# 2026-08-16 — Human player does the chores

## Ask

A human on the farm who walks over and does feed / pick / turnout / groom.

## What

- KayKit Ranger (CC0) as the owner-rider, with Idle / Walk / tool clips.
- Care buttons send you to the stall or the horse; the chore applies when you arrive and play an interact clip.
- Click the grass to walk there. Click the horse to walk over.
- One job at a time (`Still working.` if you mash buttons).

## Honest

This is a fantasy adventurer mesh, not a custom H/J rider. No pitchfork prop yet. Horse still slides to paddock after you lead them, rather than being led on a shank.
